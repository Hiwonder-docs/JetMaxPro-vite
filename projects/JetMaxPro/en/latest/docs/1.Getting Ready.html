This delicate combination helps to build intelligent picking and transferring scenarios. Through deep learning and AI vision, JetMax Pro can realize waste sorting, mask identification, object sorting, somatosensory control and many more advanced AI functions.

1.3 Lesson 3 Start the JetMax ​

NOTE

①Place robot on flat surface

②Do not drag servos forcibly, otherwise servos will be damaged.

Connect the power adaptor to the DC port on the expansion board as pictured.

1.3.1 Boot up ​

  1. Turn on expansion board. After a while, LED light on it will flash continuously. When buzzer “beeps” for three times, it means robot arm boots up successfully.

  2. After booting up, JetMax will enter AP direct connection mode by default and will launch a Wi-Fi starting with HW.

1.4 Lesson 4 Map and Device Placement ​

Place the robotic arm within the contour on the map. And the front of the robotic arm should be aligned with the front line of the contour, and the suction cups should fit the desktop tightly.

The map consists of four parts, including ①vision recognition area, ② sorting area, ③robotic arm placing area and ④ sequencing area.

① vision recognition area

The vision recognition area is the robotic arm’s visual field. The cards and blocks to be recognized need to be placed within this area

Note: please don’t place the cards or blocks in the edge of the recognition area so as to avoid recognition failure.

② Sorting area

The robotic arm will transfer the cards or blocks from the recognition area to the placing area.

The functions of above areas are as follow.

IconFunction
Stacking area
Red block placing area
Green block placing area
Blue block placing area
Recyclable waste cards placing area
Hazardous waste cards placing area
Food waste cards placing area
Residual waste cards placing area

③ Robotic arm placing area

Please ensure that the front of the robotic arm should be aligned with this line.

④ Sequencing area

The robotic arm will transfer and sequence the number and alphabet cards to this area.

1.5 Lesson 5 Position Calibration ​

The purpose of position calibration is to convert robot arm’s coordinate on the phone to its real position. After position calibration, JetMax pro can better pick and sort objects.

Note: Please strictly follow instructions below and do not skip any step.

  1. Install and connect to WonderAi APP according to ‘Lesson 4 APP Installation and Connection’. Then tap robotic icon, and select ‘position calibration’.
  1. It is normal that the image transmitted by camera delays for a few seconds. If still no image occurs after a long time, return back to home interface, then tap and enter this interface again. Once camera returned image appears, tap ’Start position calibration’

  1. Place Tag block directly under the suction nozzle following the hints. Only if the suction nozzle is at the center of the block when the robotic arm bends down, you can click “Next”.

    Note: tag block is not fixed to the dotted box. It can be placed outside the dotted box.

  1. When 5 black dots on the screen coincide with 5 colored dots on the AprilTag block, please tap “Complete”.

1.6 Lesson 6 APP Control ​

For quick user experience, select direct connection mode. In case of the choppy movement of the robot arm, you can connect to Wi-Fi of 5G band according to the file “LAN Mode Connection(Optional)”.

APP installation ​

Android user: transfer installation pack in Appendix to your phone for downloading. Note: please turn on all permissions for the APP, otherwise, robot function will be affected.

iOS user: download “WonderAi” in App Store.

Boot up JetMax ​

  1. Place JetMax on the map according to “1.Getting Ready/ Lesson 5 Map and Device Placement”.

  2. Make sure the servo wire and air pump wire are secure. (These two wires are connected before delivery)

  3. Start Jetson Nano expansion board and wait for it to boot up.

1.6.1 Device connection ​

  1. After robot starts successfully, robot will generate a WiFi starting with ‘HW’. Then open ‘WonderAi’ APP, and select ‘Advanced Kits>JetMax’
  1. Click “+” and select Direct Connection Mode.
  1. Next, follow the instruction to connect your phone to the Wi-Fi starting with “HW” in phone settings.

Note: if you operate in iOS system, please don’t return to the APP until the Wi-Fi icon appears above. Otherwise, the device cannot be searched.

  1. Having connected, go back to the APP. APP connects to the device automatically.
  1. When robotic arm icon appears, connection is completed.

1.6.2 Start the Games ​

Position Calibration ​

Before playing the AI vision games, you need to calibrate the robotic arm’s position to make it suck the object correctly. The operation is as follow.

  1. Place JetMax on the corresponding area on the map and ensure its baseboard is superimposed on the contour of robotic arm placing area.

  2. Click robotic arm icon to enter the mode selection interface, then select “Position Calibration”

  1. Click “Start Position Calibration”.
  1. According to the prompt, place the AprilTag block directly under the suction nozzle. Only if the suction nozzle is at the center of the block when the robotic arm bends down, you can click “Next”.
  1. When the 5 black dots on the screen coincide with the 5 colored dots on the AprilTag block, please click “Complete”.

APP introduction ​

  • Robot control
  1. Click JetMax icon to enter the main interface of game selection.
  1. Click “Robot Control” and select “JetMax Desktop”.
  1. The interface of “Robot Control” consists of two parts. The left side of the interface is the control area of robotic arm and suction nozzle. You can control them through the sliders and the buttons. At the right side, there is the real-time image transmitted by camera. The functions of the buttons at the left are as follow.
ButtonFunction

Used to control the motion of the robotic arm.

“Up” and “Down”: control the end of the robotic arm to move up or down.

“▲” and “▼”: control the end of the robotic arm to move forward or backward.

“◄”and “►”: control the end of the robotic arm to move left or right.

●: make the robotic arm return to the initial posture

Used to control the motion of suction nozzle

“Anti-clockwise” and “Clockwise”: control the rotation of the suction nozzle

“Lift”: the air pump starts pumping

“Release”: the air pump stops pumping and the object will be released

Used to screenshot the real-time image
**Special note: as the real-time image at the right side is mirror image, we should use the robotic arm as a reference to determine the direction when controlling the robotic arm.**

Next, we will control the robotic arm to suck a red block from the position on the left picture to the right picture.

The operation steps are as follow.

  1. Place a red block in front of the robotic arm, and the block should not be too far away from the robotic arm.

  2. Click “▲” to control the suction nozzle move directly above the block.

  3. Then press “Down” button to make the suction nozzle down to fit the block.

  4. Click “Lift” button to make the air pump start pumping.

  5. Slide the “Up” button to control the suction nozzle move up to certain height, then stop.

  6. Press “▼” button to control the robotic arm move backward.

  7. Press “Anti-clockwise” button to control the suction nozzle to move to the left side of the picture.

  8. Slide “down” button to control the suction nozzle to place the block on the desktop.

  9. Click “Release” button to make the air pump stop pumping and release the block.

  10. When the block is moved to the target position, click “●” button to make the robotic arm return to the initial posture.

  1. If you want to back to the game selection interface, you can click the blank area, then the title bar will appear. Next, click “” at the left side.
  • Waste sorting
  1. Click “Waste Sorting” to enter this game.
  1. Place the waste cards to the recognition area on the map. Switch on “Start” button. Then the robotic arm will place the waste card to the waste placing area after recognition.
  1. If you want to stop the game, switch off “Start” button.

NOTE

  1. Pay attention that the card should be parallel to the bottom line of recognition area. Otherwise, the robotic arm will place the cards incorrectly.

  2. If JetMax sucks the cards wrongly, you can click “Position Calibration”, then follow the instruction.

  • Color sorting
  1. Click “Color Sorting” to enter this game.
  1. Place the blocks to the recognition area. Then tick the color to be sorted. Lastly, switch on “Start” button to start the game. The robotic arm will place the blocks to the corresponding sorting area after recognition.
  1. If you want to quit this game, switch off “Start” button.

NOTE

  1. keep the certain interval between the blocks.

  2. If the robotic arm cannot suck the blocks correctly, you can click “Position Calibration”. Then follow the instruction to operate.

  3. If the robotic arm cannot recognize the color correctly, you can refer to the tutorial in “1. Getting Ready-> Lesson 7 Color Threshold Setting”.

  • Block stacking
  1. Click “Block Stacking” to enter the game.
  1. Place the tag blocks to the recognition area. Then click “Block Stacking” to start the game. The robotic arm will pick and stack the blocks one by one in the stacking area after recognition.
  1. If you want to quit this game, you can switch off “Start” button.

NOTE

  1. Keep the certain interval between the blocks.

  2. If the robotic arm cannot suck the blocks correctly, you can click “Position Calibration”. Then follow the instruction to operate.

  • Object tracking
  1. Click “Object Tracking” to enter the game.
  1. There are two tracking modes, namely color tracking and face tracking. Click “Instruction” to check how to install the camera under these two modes.
  1. The first mode is Color Tracking. Switch on the “Color Tracking” button to start the game. Move a colored block within the camera vision, then the robotic arm will move following the block.

  1. The second mode is Face Tracking. You need to install the camera facing forward. Switch on the “Face Tracking” button to start the game. After the game starts, the camera will move with the face.

  2. If you want to quit this game, switch off “Color Tracking” or “Face Tracking” button.

NOTE

  1. Please move the block within the camera vision and don’t move the block too fast.

  2. If the robotic arm cannot recognize the color correctly, you can refer to the tutorial in “1. Getting Ready-> Lesson 7 Color Threshold Setting”.

1.7 Lesson 7 Color Threshold Setting ​

When we playing the AI vision games, the environment light will affect the JetMax’s performance. And we can adjust the color threshold to cope with this.

Take adjusting red color for example, the operation steps are as follow.

  1. Enter the main interface, then click “” at the upper right corner to enter the interface of color threshold setting.

  2. Place the red block within the recognition area on the map, then select the red icon.

  3. Click “Instruction” button for help. According to the Lab color model diagram, red color is around “+a” zone, so we need to adjust the color threshold to “+a” zone.

  4. Click “OK” to back to the setting interface. Set the parameters of L_min, a_min and b_min as 0, and the parameters of L_max, a_max and b_max as 255.

  1. Keep the values of “a_max” unchanged, while increase the values of “a_min” until the block image in the screen turns white and other area turns black.

  1. Then based on the environment, adjust the value of L_min, L_max, b_min and b_max. If the color belongs to light red, you need to increase the value of “L_min”. If it belongs to dark red, decrease the value of “L_max”. If it belongs to dark red, decrease the value of “L_max”. If it belongs to warm tone, increase the value of “b_min”. If it belongs to cool tone, decrease the value of “b_max”
